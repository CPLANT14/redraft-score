Inside the model & score scale

Model Z = 0.50 × preseason Z + 0.40 × weekly projection Z + 0.05 × DFS salary Z + 0.05 × prop line Z. This is the final formula in your sheet, with no intercept. The same mix is used for QB, RB, WR and TE.

0-100 index = 100 × Φ(Z), where Φ is the standard-normal cumulative distribution function. Zero Z becomes 50; +1 Z becomes 84.1; +1.5 Z becomes 93.3; +2 Z becomes 97.7. This keeps order and gives a consistent scale. It is a normal-reference index, not an actual player percentile, a grade, a probability of success or a projection in points. Distances compress near the ends.

The model is calculated from the original Z-scores first, then converted. Do not average the displayed 0-100 components to reproduce it. Position ranks are actual ranks within the selected season; scores are not re-normalized when filtering or searching.

Context matters. The source model was developed to estimate next-season projection Z. This interface does not refit or recalibrate it. The sheet's final 50/40/5/5 recipe differs slightly from the earlier tested 51/40/4/5 mix; no new validation claim is made here. 2026 is a partial-season snapshot, with player-specific sample sizes. No missing source values are replaced with zero. Cross-season changes can reflect changes in the source cohorts as well as the player.
