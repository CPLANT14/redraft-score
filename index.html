Inside the model & score scale

What it is. The Redraft Score is not a prediction of how a player finishes this season. It is a better rough read on how valuable he will be next season - think of it as an upgraded points per game.

How it works. Points per game is usually the most predictive fantasy stat, so it is half the score. The other half blends four signals that each add a little more predictive power: preseason projections, weekly projections, DFS salaries and Vegas prop lines.

The weights never change: actual PPG 50% · preseason projection 25% · DFS salary 15% · weekly projection 5% · prop line 5%. If a player is missing one of those, or has played fewer than 4 games, the rest rescale to fill in - nothing is faked with zeros.

The scale. Every number is shown Madden-style from 50 to 99 against the other players at his position that season: 74.5 is average, 91.2 is strong, 98.0 is elite. Scores update daily from the live sheet.

How the stats move together

Each cell shows how strongly two stats move together, from -1.00 (exact opposite) to 1.00 (locked together). Darker color, stronger link.
